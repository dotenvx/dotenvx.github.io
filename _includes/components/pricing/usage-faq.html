Usage

What counts as an audit event?

Armor keeps a detailed audit trail of activity: decrypting with an armored keypair, navigating the UI, and making other API calls. Each recorded action counts as an audit event, helping you see who did what and when.

When does my allowance reset?

Your allowance resets at the start of each monthly billing period. Unused events do not roll over.

How much do extra events cost?

Extra events cost $0.20 per 1,000 on Solo, $0.10 on Team, and $0.025 on Business.

For example, Business includes 1 million events for $90/month. Using 1.4 million events adds $10, for a total of $100.

Do unlimited armored keys include unlimited usage?

No. You can have unlimited armored keys, but each plan has a monthly event allowance.
